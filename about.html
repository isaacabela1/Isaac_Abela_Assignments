About Us

Like you, we too are cinema lovers, and we felt the need to ease some struggles many people face at night: What movie to watch. No more endless scrolling until you lose appetite, Quick Picks provides fast and easy film suggestions.

Based in Malta we aim to provide tailored suggestions based on what’s hot at the moment. Updated weekly our recommendations stay fresh and keep you on your toes!

We aim to provide the best service possible, so for any suggestions or questions you can contact us below!
